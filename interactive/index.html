About this demo

A fixed P-BFM checkpoint runs at 50 control steps per simulated second with MuJoCo physics at 200 Hz. Slower devices play more slowly without skipping physics. Tracking uses reference motions with behavior latents from the same model's backward encoder. Built-in motions are encoded in advance; imported motions are encoded locally. Goal reaching and reward inference use exported pose and reward presets. A single connected map includes all seven training terrain families. Nominal camera placement and deterministic resets are used; this browser port is not an evaluation benchmark.

While playing, hold Shift and left-drag a body part to pull it toward the mouse. The orange line shows the pull; force is capped at 150 N. Release the mouse or Shift to stop. Pause and reset also release the robot. Push applies a horizontal 100 N force along world +Y for 0.2 simulated seconds. The orange arrow marks its direction; while paused, the push waits for Play. The reference preview stays centered on a flat floor and plays at the same reference time as the main simulation. It shows the reference pose, not another policy rollout. Goal reaching refers to a body pose, not navigation to a map location. If the robot falls, it keeps running until you pause or reset.
