RP1 training selects the reward weights/formulas and DR from the pinned
PUFORewardsCfg, PUFOEventCfg and RP1_UFO_CFG, not G1's reward/DR preset.
The nine Aux terms are action rate, contact-gated foot COM vertical velocity/
acceleration, joint acceleration/velocity, joint position limits, impact,
slippage, ankle roll and stumble. RP1 impact excludes static load; G1 is unchanged.
PD: [0.7,1.3] at reset; per-link mass/inertia: [0.8,1.2] at startup;
torso COM: +/-0.03 m at startup; friction: [0.3,1.7] at startup;
push: overwrite XY and angular velocities within +/-1 every 1--3 s, z=0.
Motor delay is one shared per-episode lag of 0/1/2 physics ticks (0/5/10 ms).
RFI is +/-10% peak torque before motor-envelope clipping at every physics tick.
Absolute PD position targets are clipped to the MJCF hard joint limits after
action scaling/default offset, before motor delay (not the soft reward limits).
Stumble takes the maximum world-frame horizontal excess across every foot
contact and all physics ticks in a control step; contacts are not top-k filtered.
No extra policy-step delay, external RFI, joint-zero offsets or forced lie-down reset.
Camera installation: +/-0.02 m in parent frame and +/-[3,10,3] degrees in camera
FLU frame, sampled at startup only. Depth crop is (18,0,14,14), output 18x36.
After sanitization/crop: range-gated 0.01 m Gaussian noise; scale [0.95,1.05]
with p=0.5; random 3x3 convolution (std=0.01) with p=0.5; dead/saturated
pixels each p=0.002 with batch p=0.5; Gaussian blur 3x3 sigma=1; normalization
of [0.1,2.5] m to [0,1]. All pixels are finally stored as uint8 in PBFM replay.
History remains 8 frames at ages 35,30,...,0, with per-episode delay 0/1 frame.

Backend differences (not an exact Newton reproduction):
PBFM uses its own MJLab 1.4/MuJoCo 3.8 backend.
It does not import the Newton-native compiled physics JSON or reproduce the
original IsaacLab/Newton simulation, dataset, networks or trained checkpoint.
MuJoCo has a single sliding-friction coefficient, not independent static and
dynamic coefficients with the source's 64 material buckets; the same range is
used but those contact semantics are not identical. Physics/contact/acceleration
signals depend on the backend. Foot COM acceleration uses MuJoCo's world-frame
inertial-frame acceleration sensor with gravity restored, not a control-step
finite difference. PBFM's terrain generation/reset placement and replay storage
remain in use. GPU rollout and short training checks are recorded in
docs/TRAINING.md; long-run policy training and hardware validation remain pending.
